# MagicDungeon

Open `project.godot` in Godot 4.6 and press F5.

Explore five procedural floors to recover the dying sanctuary flame. Start an expedition with equipment crafted at the Hearth. Enemies drop monster parts, ore deposits yield ore, and chests hold permanent equipment blueprints. Rare and Very rare designs appear on later floors and require Guard alloy (guards, floor 2+), Imp essence (imps, floor 3+) or Mire gel (slimes, floor 4+). Warden cores come from bosses. Crafting and upgrades are guaranteed when their material costs are paid. The player keeps blueprints and materials after leaving, dying or winning. At the smithy, craft equipment and upgrade the five shared equipment slots. Every item in a slot benefits from its slot level. Before every new expedition, review and select your loadout in the separate equipment screen. Dungeon levels and skill choices reset when an expedition ends. Return safely from the entrance of any floor, or continue deeper for greater rewards.

This experimental branch is a work in progress: secondary systems, interface text and balance are still being adapted.

Move with the arrow keys or WASD. E opens Equipment, C opens the journal, and hovering over the map icon shows the map. Equipment is chosen on the preparation screen and stays fixed during the expedition. The Smithy is accessible from the main menu or the Hearth. Settings lists remappable controls and audio/accessibility options.

The equipment screen shows current and prospective total stats when an item is selected; its item-stat view shows the equipment's own bonuses. Your Build shows combined stats and acquired skills. Discover elemental rune blueprints in elemental passages; the Hearth smithy can apply them to crafted gear. A rest site provides a place to recover during a floor. The west door in each floorâ€™s starting room leads to an optional side passage: choose an armory, an elemental chamber, a dangerous vault, or a restful passage. Only one side passage can be chosen on each floor.

Level-up skills respect the fixed expedition loadout: incompatible weapon skills and lifesteal synergies without vampirism are excluded. Elemental skills can still be chosen before their elemental requirements are met. Repeatable attack, defense and elemental training fill depleted choices and last only for that expedition. Older saved runs with swappable equipment retain their previous offer rules.

Bloodletter, Winterblade and Caustic brand masteries unlock guaranteed smithy blueprints, including for characters who completed them before the crafting system was added. Colossus axe and greatblade blueprints can be found in matching Rare-or-better rewards from floor 3 onward. These five Rare special weapons cost 3 Ore, 2 Parts and 1 Warden core, use the shared weapon-slot level, and accept elemental runes. Daily expeditions retain their fixed sword and do not advance mastery; practice expeditions do not advance mastery either.

`PLAYTEST_GUIDE.md` contains the current external-test brief. `ASSET_CREDITS.md` records asset licenses. The other design documents describe earlier iterations and may contain historical mechanics; use the game and current playtest guide as the source of truth for present behavior.

Run a regression script with `Godot --headless --path . --script res://tests/onboarding_controls.gd`, or use the other scripts under `tests/`.

## The Last Hearth

Choose **Hearth** on the title or run-end screen to visit the sanctuary. Find Mara, Iven and manuscripts in the dungeon; discoveries and Warden fragments are carried home at the end of a standard run, including after death. Their unlocks apply to the next run. The Hearth journal shows the locations and rewards. Reaching the final exit presents a story choice, and a victory unlocks optional Vows. New expeditions currently offer the five-floor Dungeon only; Daily and Endless are temporarily unavailable. Existing active saves remain readable.

Existing characters retain their previous unlocks; their sanctuary starts with the discovery-based options available. Standard new characters begin with the ordinary weapons, equipment and skill pool, with four advanced elemental skills unlocked by manuscripts. The existing Ember Tree remains available; Tinkering now adds a defensive-item choice to Mara's armory service.

Each floor’s main rest site now offers one immediate expedition reward: a compatible skill, +1 elemental power, or a healing flask. Its options stay fixed when revisiting or loading a save. An already acquired offered skill becomes repeatable attack training. Elemental passage guardians also grant +1 matching elemental power once per floor, alongside their permanent rune-blueprint reward. These bonuses reset when the expedition ends.

Newly prepared crafted weapon families have stronger identities: Keen adds 5–7% critical chance and +0.30–0.40 critical multiplier across slot levels 1–5; Swift adds 12–16% attack speed with 5% less item attack; Bloodbound grants 6–8% lifesteal with 10% less Common item attack (daggers also lose 1.5 percentage points of evasion). Existing expedition snapshots retain their old family bonuses. Armor, amulets and special weapons use their existing rules. `tests/weapon_family_balance.gd` compares old and new families at levels 1, 3 and 5 across short, armored, critical, elemental and sequential encounters; its JSON report is written to `work/weapon_family_balance.json`. These simulations are balance probes, not human win-rate estimates.

Returning safely after defeating at least one Warden grants a 10% bonus on banked expedition embers (rounded down, minimum 1). The confirmation shows the exact bonus; victory also qualifies, including its ordinary victory embers. First-clear Threat rewards are separate. Death retains the existing blueprints, materials and collected embers but grants no safe-return bonus. Daily and practice runs do not qualify. Quartermaster and Iron Cache now add their ore to the first deposit mined per expedition instead of granting ore at the start; the claim persists across floors and save/load.

Combat playback can be switched between 1× and 2× using the fight-panel button, Enter/Space, or controller A. Each encounter and resumed fight starts at 1×; health at or below 35% returns playback to 1×. Flasks remain available with the configured Heal key or controller left shoulder. The panel shows progress toward the next weapon ability; a separate notice prioritizes freezes, armor breaks, shields and combo resets, including with Calm effects enabled. Combat recaps show damage absorbed by shields and the portion supplied by Sword ward; shields from older saves without source information are counted only toward the overall shield total.

New characters receive optional guidance through inspecting a weapon, discovering and crafting an alternative, equipping it, choosing a tracked crafting goal, and seeing a weapon ability trigger. Their first floor-1 victory awards a Dagger blueprint, 3 Ore and 1 extra Part, making that first alternative affordable with the normal enemy drop. The reward cannot repeat once an alternative weapon blueprint is known or carried. Milestones persist per character, can be skipped with F1 or the on-screen action, and never restrict progression. Established characters are not enrolled automatically. The title and run-end screens prominently lead to the Hearth, where the current learning step offers a direct route to crafting or equipment preparation. Daily and practice expeditions do not advance this guidance.

Each floor now emphasizes its own enemy family, with themed enemies spread through non-boss rooms and excluded from entrance rooms, boss rooms and gatekeepers. A marked preparation site on every floor offers a countermeasure or supplies: weaken the Keep boss; drain Archive cisterns to reduce Leech healing and Tide follow-ups; douse Crypt braziers to halve regeneration; sabotage the Foundry to reduce Construct plates and slow Furnace Heat; light Sanctum beacons to weaken Shade dodges and boss resistance. Preparation affects existing and later-created enemies on that floor, persists through saving, and resets on the next floor. Its alternative is 1 Ore and 1 flask. The main expedition reward now offers a floor-relevant elemental blessing (Acid, Frost, Fire, Acid, Frost). Frost, shielding, repeated hits, critical hits and heavy attacks remain independent counters. `tests/floor_identity.gd` checks placement, real combat effects and save behavior and writes controlled boss probes to `work/floor_identity_audit.json`; these probes do not establish full-run balance.

Validation tooling and the recorded Standard campaign baseline are documented in `VALIDATION_GUIDE.md`. Run `python tools/run_regressions.py` for current regression checks or add `--all` for historical contracts. `PLAYTEST_GUIDE.md` describes the human sessions still to conduct. Reports export locally from Settings and include crafting, skill-choice and encounter metrics.
