# Element specialization (run rules 17)

The new rules apply only to new runs. A saved fight keeps the rules with which it started. Equipment and skills contribute **power**; Frost, Acid and Poison power is converted to counters per hit using the weapon's base attack interval. This preserves the earlier dagger/sword/axe application-rate balancing. Mastery is included in power before checking the thresholds below.

- **Frost:** each counter slows enemy attacks by 2.5%, to 50%. At 20 counters, freeze for one second, then return to 10 counters. The usual cooldown is eight seconds; Winter Bell reduces it to four. At 4 Frost power, a freeze also deals Frost damage, scaling with power and the weapon's base interval (up to 10 normalized hits). Frost Shatter now empowers the next successful heavy hit after a freeze by 100%, consumes 10 counters, and ends any remaining freeze. The charge persists until the next successful heavy hit, so slow weapons can use it.
- **Fire:** the strongest burn remains active and ignores armor. At 4 Fire power, hits on a burning target add Heat: +10% burn damage each, up to 4 Heat. At 8 power, the limit is 8. Axes and greatblades add 2 Heat per hit; other weapons add 1. Heat ends when the burn expires. Combustion is a Rare heavy-weapon skill: at 4 Heat, the next successful hit consumes Heat and deals a Fire burst equal to twice the current burn damage.
- **Poison:** each counter deals 0.25 damage every 0.5 seconds, ignoring armor. This is half the old baseline, to make space for specialization bursts. At 4 Poison power, hits triggering every crossed 10-counter threshold deal a burst of twice Poison power. Toxic Harvest is a Rare skill that doubles those bursts. Poison still accumulates for the fight; it does not heal through vampirism.
- **Acid:** each counter removes 1 armor. Once armor is gone, excess counters add 1% physical damage, to 20%. The limit rises to 40% at 4 Acid power and 80% at 8. At 4 power, the hit that removes remaining armor also deals an Acid burst, scaling with power and the weapon's base interval (up to 15 normalized hits). Exposed Core is a Rare skill granting +25% physical hit power after armor is broken.

Frost and Acid burst damage is normalized for the weapon's original interval, as their application rate is. Fire and Poison damage and these bursts ignore armor; physical-only weapon multipliers do not increase Frost or Acid bursts. Existing two-element skills—including Toxic Glacier (Poison ticks add Frost) and Corrosive Embers (burns damage acid-affected enemies)—remain in the random eligible pool. The new skills require the corresponding power and, for Combustion, a heavy weapon. They do not bypass the normal rarity and offer rules.

The build details show power, counters per hit and current burst values; level-up previews flag power thresholds. Combat feedback shows Heat, counters and bursts. The skill and elemental rules use the same saved battle state and deterministic prediction.

A controlled 500-HP, 20-armor comparison with equal total elemental investment found a tradeoff for all four focused builds against a 2/2/2/2 split with dagger, sword and axe: Fire, Acid and Poison killed sooner, while Frost took less damage. This single synthetic encounter does not establish full-run balance or prove all combinations are equal; human playtesting is still required.
